In 2001, seventeen people wrote one page. Twenty-five years later, an industry sells in its name what that page refused, and software has never been so heavy, so slow and so fragile.

Froment Software publishes its position: [How to make software](https://how.froment.software/latest).

## Three sentences

- **People answer.** A change has an author, a role has a plain name, a captain holds the course, and nobody needs a ritual to know when to work.
- **The repository is the truth.** Documentation, decisions and infrastructure live beside the code. Every action has a number, and any version can be rebuilt byte for byte.
- **Measurements judge.** Measure the system, never the person. Automatic gates refuse what is not up for debate, and dates come from the past.

## The text

Twenty-five positions, their sources and an order of adoption, in English and French, for products that several teams work on. Every edition is dated and kept.
